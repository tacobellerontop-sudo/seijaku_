settings

A few settings. Everything else is already decided: quiet, light, and out of the way.

Everything else in a custom theme stays Ink, so it can't come out unreadable.

privacy

Tracking protection is the engine's own blocker. For stronger ad blocking, install uBlock Origin from the extensions page — it blocks more, and it is optional here on purpose.

Clear browsing data

Clearing cookies signs you out of sites. Files you downloaded stay where they are.

downloads

Files downloaded from pages land here, and so do annotated PDFs and bookmark exports. Changing it affects new downloads only — anything already downloaded stays where it is.

protected video

Netflix, Disney+, Spotify and the like play through Google's Widevine, downloaded from Mozilla (about 10 MB) when you press the button. The browser's automatic check never runs on its own here, so this button asks for it now instead of waiting.

Pages that already asked for protected video need a reload.

weather

The home page's weather widget, from Open-Meteo (no account, no key). Changing the city re-geocodes it on the next home page load.

shortcuts

Every shortcut Seijaku answers.

  • Ctrl+T — new tab, with the floating bar open
  • Ctrl+L — floating bar for this tab
  • Ctrl+K — command palette (commands, tabs, bookmarks)
  • Ctrl+W — close tab
  • Ctrl+Shift+T — reopen closed tab
  • Ctrl+Shift+N — private window
  • Ctrl+Shift+S — sleep this tab now
  • Ctrl+Shift+P — pin or unpin this tab
  • Ctrl+Shift+H — hide or show the strip
  • Ctrl+D — bookmark this page
  • Ctrl+Shift+B — bookmarks
  • Ctrl+H — history
  • Ctrl+J — downloads
  • Ctrl+F — find in page
  • Ctrl+= / Ctrl+− / Ctrl+0 — zoom in, out, reset
  • Ctrl+R — reload
  • Alt+Left / Alt+Right — back, forward
  • Ctrl+, — settings
