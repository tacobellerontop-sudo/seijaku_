# Seijaku 1.0.2

## PDFs open again

PDFs now open in a tab, in Firefox's own PDF viewer: a PDF link you click on a page, a PDF address you type or bookmark, and a PDF file on your computer. Before this, most PDFs went straight to your downloads instead.

The viewer can draw, highlight, add text and fill in forms. Its Download button and Ctrl+S save a copy with your changes. Ctrl+F searches the PDF, and Ctrl+plus, Ctrl+minus and Ctrl+0 zoom it.

This replaces Seijaku's own PDF page, which only opened PDFs you typed in. Old bookmarks and history entries that point at it now open the PDF itself. The package is also about 3 MB smaller, because the viewer already ships inside the engine.
