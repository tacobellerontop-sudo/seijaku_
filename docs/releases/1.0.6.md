# Seijaku 1.0.6

## More sign-in buttons work

"Sign in with Google" and similar buttons often sit inside a small frame from the sign-in site rather than on the page itself. Those buttons now open their sign-in tab too, and the login finishes back on the page.

## Blocked pop-ups are no longer silent

When a page tries to open a pop-up without a click, the pop-up blocker stops it. Before, nothing happened at all. Now the line at the bottom of the window says the page tried to open a pop-up, with an Open it button.
